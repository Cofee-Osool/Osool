 The Bean Journey

From Seed to Sip

We believe great coffee begins long before the roaster. Follow the journey that transforms a humble seed into your perfect cup.

— 01 —

Farming

We partner directly with smallholder farmers in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Yemen — paying fair premiums for hand-picked, sun-ripened cherries grown at altitude.

— 02 —

Roasting

Our head roaster profiles each origin individually, using precision drum roasting to reveal the bean's true character — never masking, always enhancing.

— 03 —

Brewing

Whether pour-over, espresso, or cold brew — our baristas are trained in the art of extraction, ensuring every cup achieves its full flavour potential.
